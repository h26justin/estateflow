2. Arthur Online

Arthur Online

From £65/month
Best for letting agents and 50+ unit portfolios

Arthur Online has been around since 2015 and is the closest UK product to the US-style enterprise property management platforms (AppFolio, Buildium). Strong workflows for letting agents managing other people's properties, with trust accounting, agent-side billing and approval chains. Probably overkill below 30-40 units.

    Strengths
  • Mature, used by hundreds of letting agents
  • Trust accounting and agent invoicing
  • Strong workflow approvals (maintenance, tenancy changes)
  • Comprehensive tenant portal with chat

    Limitations
  • Pricing starts high (£65/mo minimum) — bad fit under 30 units
  • MTD ITSA not yet live (on roadmap)
  • Add-ons (Right to Rent, referencing) push monthly cost up significantly
  • Onboarding takes weeks for non-agency users

Landlord Vision sits between Properly and Arthur — pitched at accountants and accountant-shy landlords who want strong reporting and tax integration. Good Section 24 implementation and one of the few with an MTD ITSA add-on already shipping. UI is more dated than the newer entrants.

Decision framework — which one fits you

A simple way to think about this:

  • 1-3 properties, personal name, MTD ITSA looming → Properly (cheapest with full MTD)
  • 4-25 properties, mix of personal and SPV → Properly (multi-company billing built in)
  • 25-50 properties, growing fast → Properly works, but evaluate Landlord Vision if your accountant prefers it
  • 50+ units, employing staff or managing for clients → Arthur Online (built for this scale)
  • Heavy refinancer / portfolio investor → Lendlord for analysis, paired with Properly for ongoing management
  • Just need to advertise a property → OpenRent (one-off fee, not a subscription)

How to switch between tools

Switching landlord software is easier than switching accounting software. Almost everything you need lives in three CSV exports:

  1. Properties — name, address, value, mortgage, rent. Every tool can import this.
  2. Tenants and tenancies — name, dates, deposit, rent. Most tools accept a standard CSV format.
  3. Compliance dates — Gas Safety, EICR, EPC expiry. These are just dates — type them in, no migration needed.

Historical rent payments are the only thing that's painful to migrate. Most landlords decide not to bother and start the new tool fresh from the current year. Your accountant has the old year's records.

If you want to try Properly, the bulk-add wizard handles a block of properties in one step, and you can be set up in about 15 minutes. The 14-day free trial doesn't ask for a card — if it isn't an upgrade on what you have, walk away.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best landlord software in the UK?

The best UK landlord software depends on portfolio size and budget. For 1-25 properties with MTD ITSA needs, Properly is the most affordable comprehensive option at £2 per property per month. For agency-scale (50+ units) Arthur Online has the deepest workflows but is much more expensive. Landlord Vision sits in between.

How much should I pay for landlord software?

For 1-25 properties, expect £10-£50/month total. For 25-100 properties, £50-£200/month is typical. Above that you're in agency-tier (£200-£2,000/month). Per-property pricing (e.g. Properly at £2/property) scales most predictably; flat-fee tools become poor value once you outgrow the bundled feature limits.

Do I need landlord software or are spreadsheets enough?

Spreadsheets work for 1-2 properties if you are disciplined, but from April 2026 spreadsheets alone cannot file MTD ITSA submissions. Above 3 properties most landlords find compliance-reminder automation alone justifies the cost. The biggest savings come from missing fewer Gas Safety / EICR expiry deadlines, which carry fines up to £30,000.

Is Arthur Online or Landlord Vision better?

Arthur Online is built for letting agents and high-end portfolio landlords (50+ properties). Landlord Vision targets accountants and smaller portfolio landlords (5-50 properties) with strong reporting. For under 25 properties, both are typically more expensive than necessary. Properly fits the gap with per-property pricing.

Which landlord software is best for MTD ITSA?

MTD ITSA goes live April 2026. As of May 2026, Properly has the most complete MTD ITSA workflow with HMRC OAuth, fraud-prevention headers, sandbox practice mode and Section 24 calculations baked in. Landlord Vision has an MTD add-on. Most others have it on their roadmap but no working integration yet.

Can I switch landlord software easily?

Yes. Most UK landlord platforms allow CSV import of property + tenant data, and Properly has a bulk-add wizard for blocks of properties. Compliance certificate dates can be migrated by typing the expiry date — there is rarely a vendor lock-in beyond historical rent payment data. Switch in an afternoon if the current tool is not working for you.
